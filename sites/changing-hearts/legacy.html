What if my loved one has memory loss?

We adapt: shorter sessions, photo prompts, and family members in the room. With a legal representative's consent, we can also build the story from family recollections.

Who sees the finished story?

Only your family, unless you choose otherwise. We never publish a legacy story without separate, written permission.

Can it be a gift?

Yes. Many families give it for a birthday, anniversary or holiday. We can send a beautiful gift card announcing it.

How long does it take?

Usually three to six weeks from the interview, depending on the package. Tell us if you have a date in mind.
