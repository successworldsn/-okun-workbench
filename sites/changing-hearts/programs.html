Home/Programs

Three ways to make care felt.

Each program is simple to say yes to. We bring the people, the materials and the plan; your community brings the welcome. Every program can be one-time or ongoing.

01 / Read

The Reading Room

Books and shared reading moments that invite curiosity and connection. Large-print titles, read-aloud sessions and conversation circles, chosen with residents, not for them.

  • A curated shelf of large-print and audio-friendly books for the community to keep
  • Hosted read-aloud and discussion sessions
  • Intergenerational reading with students and local volunteers
  • A short recap for staff and partners after each session
02 / Welcome

The Welcome Moment

Moving into a care community is one of life's biggest changes. Thoughtful welcome packs and small gestures help people feel at home, and help families feel they chose well.

  • A beautifully made welcome pack with comfort items and a personal note
  • A "getting to know you" card that helps staff connect from day one
  • A first-week check-in moment
  • Packs can carry a partner's name, tastefully
03 / Connect

The Story Corner

Creative activities and shared stories that make room for belonging. Memory prompts, music, photo sharing and gentle interviews, where a life story finally gets the audience it deserves.

  • Guided storytelling and memory-sharing sessions
  • Creative activities: letters, music, photo walls
  • With consent, stories can become a keepsake for families
  • Never filmed or published without permission

How a program starts

From hello to first moment.

A short conversation

Tell us about your community: who lives there, what they enjoy, what your team needs.

A simple plan

We propose a program, a date and a clear list of who does what. No cost to the community when a partner sponsors it.

The moment

Our team arrives prepared, runs the program and leaves the space as we found it, only warmer.

The recap

Staff and partners get a concise summary of what was delivered and what's next.

Questions

Good to know.

What does a program cost a care community?

When a local partner sponsors the program, nothing. Communities can also book programs directly; ask us for current pricing.

Who runs the sessions?

Our team and trained, background-checked volunteers. We coordinate with your activities staff so every session fits your schedule and your residents.

Do you photograph or film residents?

Only with written consent from the resident or their legal representative, and with your community's approval. Anyone can opt out at any time.

How far do you travel?

We are growing community by community. Tell us where you are and we'll let you know honestly whether we can serve you now or soon.
